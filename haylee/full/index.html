Questions to ask on tours

  • What's included: water, trash, internet, parking?
  • Is there a garage or covered spot? (Iowa winters.)
  • Laundry in the unit or shared?
  • Lease length options, and the move-in date?
  • How are maintenance requests handled, and how fast?
  • Pet policy and fees, even if just someday.

Apartment hunting cheat sheet

West Des Moines / Jordan Creek
Newer complexes, shopping, quiet · $$		1BR roughly $1,000–1,250. Easy parking, everything in 10 min.
Waukee
Newest suburb, growing fast · $$		Lots of brand-new buildings; a bit farther from downtown.
Clive / Urbandale
Established suburbs · $–$$		Mix of older and newer complexes; near I-35/80.
Downtown / East Village
Walkable, lofts, nightlife · $$–$$$		Ask about parking costs and noise on weekend nights.
Ingersoll / Sherman Hill
Old houses split into apartments · $–$$		Lots of character; check heat, laundry and parking.
Drake
Student neighborhood · $		Cheaper; leases often follow the school year.
Johnston / Ankeny
Family suburbs to the north · $$		Nice and newer, but 20+ min to West Des Moines.

My notes

Getting around

  • Two rivers. The Raccoon runs along the bottom, just south of where we start, near home. The Des Moines River comes down from the top. They meet just south of downtown, where the city started.
  • I-235, the spine. Straight through the middle, east to west. Almost everywhere is about 5 minutes from it. Locals say "north of 235" or "south of 235."
  • 35/80, the ring. Wraps the top and left edges like an upside-down L. Johnston and Ankeny are north; Clive and Waukee are west.
  • Street numbers. Bigger number, farther west. 63rd Street is the line between West Des Moines and Des Moines.
